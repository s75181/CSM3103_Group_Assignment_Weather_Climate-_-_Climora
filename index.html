ℹ️ About Climora

What is Climora?

Climora is a smart mobile solution powered by Data.gov.my. We bridge the gap between raw government meteorological data and actionable, eco-friendly living habits. Our mission is to empower students and the public to stay prepared for weather changes while maintaining sustainable lifestyles.
